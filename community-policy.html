Your words. Your credit.

Community policy · September 27, 2026

What you submit

We save your word, part of speech, definition, optional pen name, submission time and permission confirmation. Do not include private contact details or other sensitive information. No player account is required. Only approved entries are public.

Your permission

You retain ownership of your original writing. By checking the submission box, you give The Devil’s Dictionary non-exclusive permission to display your submission in its game and in promotional share cards with your chosen pen name, or anonymously. Publication is optional and unpaid. We will not label your contribution as Ambrose Bierce’s work.

Moderation and removal

We may reject or remove copied content, targeted abuse, private information, spam or unsuitable material. Use Report Entry to flag a published definition. To request removal of your own work, email support@devildictionary.com with the entry link and details that help us verify your request. Copies already downloaded or shared by others may remain outside our control.

Storage and retention

Submissions and reports are stored on our server-side database. Hosting providers process request information to deliver the service. We use an hourly keyed digest of your network address to limit abuse; raw network addresses are not saved in the community database. Expired rate-limit records are cleared during later requests. Pending submissions are kept up to 90 days; rejected and removed entries and resolved reports are cleared after 30 days during later requests. Published entries and unresolved reports remain until removed or resolved. Hosting logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

Cookies

Players do not need a login cookie. The private editor’s desk uses a one-hour authentication cookie. Original-game progress continues to stay on your own device.
