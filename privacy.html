Privacy

Updated 24 October 2026.

Original game

Your game progress is saved locally on your device. Playing the original game does not require an account.

Site measurement

The public website loads Vercel Web Analytics. Vercel describes this product as aggregated measurement that does not use third-party cookies. A visitor is identified by a hash of the incoming request, not by an account, and that visitor session is discarded after 24 hours. The published data is not tied to an individual, a customer, or an IP address, and it is not used to follow you across other websites. See Vercel’s analytics privacy policy.

Custom events record starting a game, finishing a word, sharing, opening a social profile, or clicking a purchase button. Those events can include the public dictionary word and a coarse label such as “x” or “checkout”. They do not include your email address or the text of a definition.

The public website also loads the Meta Pixel (ID 1832150057779347) from connect.facebook.net. The script is asynchronous. It sends Meta a PageView for each page load, a Lead event when a daily-letter signup is accepted, a custom StartPlay event when a game starts, and a custom CompleteWord event when a word is finished. Those events can include the public dictionary word. They do not include your email address or the text of a definition. Meta may set a cookie such as _fbp and use the events to measure and deliver advertising. See Meta’s privacy policy.

The iOS app does not load Vercel Web Analytics or the Meta Pixel. The private editor still uses its own authentication cookie, described below. That cookie is separate from analytics.

Daily letter

If you submit the daily-word form, this site sends that address to Resend, which keeps the list and sends one letter each morning. The letter contains the day’s word, its definition, and links back to this site. The address is not written to this site’s logs, it is not included in analytics events, and it is not sold. The form does not put you on the list until Resend accepts the address.

Each letter includes an unsubscribe link, which stops further messages. You can also ask to be removed by writing to support@devildictionary.com.

Optional community features

If you submit a definition or report, we store the information you send so the editor can review it. Approved entries and pen names are public. The private editor uses an authentication cookie. See the community submissions and privacy policy for collected fields, permission, retention and removal requests.

Optional purchases

Creem processes checkout and delivers purchased files. Information entered at checkout is handled by Creem under its privacy policy. We may use order details to provide purchase support. Do not send payment card details to our support address.

Hosting and correspondence

Vercel handles request information, including network addresses, to serve this website. Hosting logs are subject to the provider’s practices. If you email support, we retain the correspondence to respond to you. Do not send sensitive personal information in submissions or reports.

Contact and requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact support@devildictionary.com. Include the relevant entry link. We do not sell your submissions or contact details.
