FROM DOWNLOAD TO TABLETOP

Print. Cut. Pass it around.

  1. Choose your paper. A4 or US Letter. All cards and rules are in English.
  2. Print at 100%. Use portrait, Actual Size and long-edge flip for the card pages. Test one sheet: matching numbers belong back-to-back.
  3. No duplex printer? Print single-sided, cut along the borders and glue matching numbered cards together.
  4. Playing the family game? For the free family starter, print an extra copy of page 3 for each scoring player. Keep the backs and answer key with the host.

Bring pencils and spare paper. White backgrounds keep ink use low. Printer alignment varies, so try plain paper before using heavier stock.
