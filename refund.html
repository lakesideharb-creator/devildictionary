Refund Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026 · Effective 14 September 2026

The short version. Nothing in this game costs money, so nothing can be refunded. There is no paid tier, no in-app purchase, no subscription and no unlock — on the website or on iOS. If you ever see a charge claiming to be from us, it is not.

1. What costs money

Nothing. devildictionary.com is free to play, takes no payment details, has no account step, and sells nothing. The complete edition — all 100 entries and all four stages — is playable at no charge. The iOS edition is the same: free to install, with nothing to buy inside it. See the pricing page for the full picture.

2. Why there is a refund page at all

Because a store listing asks for one, and because a promise written down is worth more than a promise implied. The policy is simply this: you cannot be charged, so you cannot need your money back.

  • No in-app purchases exist. There is no unlock, no restore button, and no paywall.
  • No subscription exists, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing that renews.
  • Devil Coins cannot be bought at any price. They are earned by solving words and they have no monetary value.

3. If something ever charges you

It did not come from us. We hold no payment information and process no transactions, so no charge can legitimately originate from this game. Check your statement for the merchant name, and if anything claims to be The Devil’s Dictionary, write to support@devildictionary.com so we can look into it. Never include your password, full card number, or any verification code; we do not need them and you should not send them.

4. There is nothing to cancel

No free trial converts into a subscription. No auto-renewal exists anywhere in this product. No promotional pricing is scheduled. Everything you can play today stays playable, at no cost, for as long as the site is up.

5. Changes to this policy

Any change will be reflected in the date above. We will not start charging for the 100 entries that are free today, and we will not reduce any refund right you had at the time you used the game.

6. Contact

An unexplained charge, or a plain complaint: support@devildictionary.com.
