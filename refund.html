Refund Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026 · Effective 14 September 2026

The short version. The version you can play right now on this website is free, so there is nothing to refund. When the paid mobile edition exists, the app store processes your payment — which means the app store is also the only party that can return it. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but we will never argue with a reasonable request, and we will help you word it.

1. What currently costs money

Nothing on this website does. devildictionary.com is free to play, takes no payment details, has no account step, and sells nothing. The complete web edition — all 45 entries and every stage — is playable at no charge. See the pricing page for what will change when the mobile edition is released.

2. The one thing you will be able to buy

The mobile edition offers a single product: “The Devil’s Dictionary — Complete Edition”, priced at $4.99 USD, a one-time purchase that permanently unlocks the full entry list.

  • It is not a subscription. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • It is not consumable. Once unlocked, it stays unlocked.
  • Devil Coins cannot be bought at any price. They are earned by solving words and they have no monetary value.

3. How to request a refund

Purchases made in the App Store are billed by Apple. Apple holds your payment information; we do not. For that reason only Apple can issue the refund — no developer can.

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Sign in with the Apple ID you used to make the purchase.
  3. Find the transaction and choose Report a Problem or Request a refund.
  4. Select a reason, add a sentence about what went wrong, and submit.

Apple normally replies within about 48 hours and will let you know the outcome by email. Apple generally accepts refund requests made within 90 days of the purchase date, though the exact window, the number of requests permitted, and the final decision vary by country and remain at Apple’s discretion.

We cannot issue refunds, cancel charges, or reverse transactions ourselves. If anyone purporting to represent us offers to process a direct payment or refund outside the App Store, it is not us.

4. We will back you up

Although we cannot press the refund button, we do not hide behind that. Write to support@devildictionary.com and tell us what went wrong — a crash, a purchase that did not unlock, a description that misled you. Include the approximate date of the purchase and the country of your app store account. Never include your password, full card number, or Apple ID verification codes; we do not need them and you should not send them.

If something is genuinely broken, we would rather refund you than pretend otherwise.

5. Already paid and lost access? Do not buy it again

If you paid and the unlock has disappeared — after moving to a new phone, reinstalling, restoring from a backup, or signing in with a different Apple ID — use Restore Purchases inside the app. It re-checks your entitlement with Apple and turns everything back on at no cost.

Only if restoring fails should you contact us. Buying a second time never restores anything; it just charges you twice.

6. There is nothing to cancel

No free trial converts into a subscription. No auto-renewal exists anywhere in this product. No promotional pricing is scheduled. Once you own the Complete Edition you keep it even if we later change the price for new buyers.

7. Changes to this policy

Any change will be reflected in the date above. We will not reduce refund options that were available to you at the time you purchased.

8. Contact

Refund support, restore problems, or a plain complaint: support@devildictionary.com.
